# Power BI Sales Analytics & Executive Dashboard

[![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Power%20BI-Analytics-F2C811?style=for-the-badge&logo=powerbi&logoColor=black)](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/)
[![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-Data%20Gen-3776AB?style=for-the-badge&logo=python&logoColor=white)](https://www.python.org/)
[![Pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-Data-150458?style=for-the-badge&logo=pandas&logoColor=white)](https://pandas.pydata.org/)

An interactive Power BI Business Intelligence project featuring a realistic synthetic e-commerce sales generator script (`generate_data.py`) and executive dashboard model designed to track sales revenue, regional performance, product categories, and profit margins.

---

## 📊 Business Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue & Gross Profit Margin Tracking**
- **Regional Sales Distribution & Territory Performance**
- **Monthly Revenue Growth & Trend Forecasting**
- **Top Performing Product Categories & Order Quantities**

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Data Workflow

```
[generate_data.py] ──► [Excel Data Model (Data/sales_data.xlsx)]
                                   │
                                   ▼
                   [Power BI Interactive Dashboard]
                     (PowerBI/powerbi-sales.pbix)
```
